# 4. Independent Payout Months for Split Share Partners

We decided that a Share may be divided among up to 4 partners whose combined contributions equal 100% of a monthly share, and each partner may select an independent Payout Turn month. To guarantee mathematical solvency, the system enforces a strict monthly invariant: the sum of payout fractions allocated to each month must equal exactly 100% (1.0 full pot) before a Gameya can become Active.
